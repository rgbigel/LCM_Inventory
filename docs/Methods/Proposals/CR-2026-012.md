# CR-2026-012: Implement LCM Two-Tier Proposal and Review Governance Engine

- **Origin Repository**: `Workspace_Inventory`
- **Target LCM Version**: `v4.1.0`
- **Status**: **implemented**
- **Bundle**: `BUNDLE-2026-03`
- **Author**: Rolf & Antigravity AI Assistant

## Summary
Implements the LCM Two-Tier Proposal and Review Governance Engine:
1. **Tier 1 (Intent & Proposals)**: Captures questions and ideas as non-executing `suggested` proposals until explicitly approved with `do <all, #n, #n-#m> Proposals`. Supports batch actions, `delete`, `defer`, and configurable review granularity (`coarse` default vs `tight`).
2. **Tier 2 (Repository Review Queue)**: Tracks repositories under visual Beyond Compare 5 review with `give repos under review`, supports per-repository commit deferral, and enforces dual-commit & push synchronization between target repositories and `Workspace_Inventory`.
3. **Workspace Invariant**: `Workspace_Inventory` is the authoritative persistent store for all proposals (`data/proposals/proposals.json`) and is exempt from visual BC5 review.
