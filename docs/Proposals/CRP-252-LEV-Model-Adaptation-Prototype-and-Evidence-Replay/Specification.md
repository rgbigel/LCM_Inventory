# CRP-252: LEV Model Adaptation Prototype and Evidence Replay

| Metadata Field | Value |
|---|---|

| **Origin Repository** | `LCM_Inventory` |

## Objective

Evaluate adaptation of the LEV AI model into LCM internal and external tools
through a sandboxed prototype.

## Mandatory Evidence Replay

The prior Gemini analysis of LEV must be repeated and exploited as prototype
evidence. The replay must identify which conclusions are verified against the
LEV model, provider documentation, runtime behavior, and LCM constraints;
unverified conclusions remain labelled as gaps.

## Pending Inputs

- Exact LEV provider, model identity, version, deployment, and access method.
- Sandbox boundary, data policy, credential handling, and tool permissions.
- Intended LCM roles and internal/external integration candidates.
- Functional, quality, latency, cost, privacy, and promotion criteria.

## Non-Goals

- Do not integrate LEV into live LCM tooling before prototype promotion.
- Do not rely on Gemini-derived claims without replay and adaptation evidence.
